# Factory & Product Video Background

Place your official company factory & product video file here:

File path: `public/videos/factory-hero.mp4`

Requirements for optimal web performance:
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264 video codec, AAC audio codec if audio included)
- **Resolution**: 1080p (1920x1080) or 720p (1280x720)
- **Bitrate**: 3–5 Mbps (compressed for fast web streaming)
- **Duration**: 15–45 seconds looping video

The homepage hero will automatically detect and play `/videos/factory-hero.mp4` on autoplay loop!
